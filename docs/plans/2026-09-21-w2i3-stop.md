## 1. System overview

Ending a round on purpose loses it today: `done`/`unbind` SIGTERM a
headless process (`stopProcess`, `headless.go:566`) and abandon a pane
builder mid-plan; the round budget halt and a switch do the same. Nothing
gives the builder the one thing that makes the round recoverable -- a
chance to commit and write what it got to. `relay stop` does, for pane
builders: it types a wrap-up prompt through the same delivery path as the
plan, records `StopRequestedAt`/grace on the binding, shows `stopping 1m20s
of 5m` in status, and lets the daemon close the round on the marker as
usual (the report entry note gains `stopped`). Grace elapsed with no marker
-> `NEEDS YOU: builder did not stop within 5m; close its pane yourself`
(relay does not kill panes). Headless builders have no stdin, so `stop` on
a headless round is design question 1's option (a): kill now, then **close
the round without a report** (`noreport stopped`) instead of the
exit-without-report path that would switch builders -- a stop is not a
failure. Option (b), a one-shot wrap-up process, is deferred (say so in
README). `--now` skips the prompt for panes too (abandon at once). `done`
and `unbind` keep today's immediate behaviour (the `--grace` variants from
the issue are not in this round); design question 2 (interrupt keys) is
not in this round. Send and round close clear the stop bookkeeping.

## 6. Error handling

- Prompt failure on a pane stop returns the prompt error and records
  nothing (the human can retry or `--now`).
- Kill failure on a headless stop returns `ErrStopFailed`-shaped text as
  `done` does and leaves the round open.
- A stop never charges `RoundSwitches` and never excludes a candidate.
