## 1. System overview

#132 runs a *command* at round close and records pass/fail; this runs a
*model* and records a verdict. Both annotate, neither decides. The pieces
exist: headless consults (`relay ask --headless`: `internal/relay/ask.go`
`reserveConsult`/`recordConsult`, the `Runner.Start` launch, the
`reconcileConsults` headless branch that turns the final message into
`FindingsPath` and queues `KindFindings`), the #133 block parser
(`internal/relay/reporttail.go` `ParseReportTail`, a ```` ```relay ````
fenced block of `key: value` lines), the gate record on the report entry.
After this round: `relay send --verify` (or `policy.json` `verify.default:
true`) marks the round; when it closes -- after the gate, so the reviewer
sees its output -- relay creates a **detached throwaway worktree at the
builder's HEAD** (design: the reviewer may run tests without touching the
builder's or the planner's tree), composes the verification question
itself, and launches the reviewer candidate (`resolveCandidate(...,
"reviewer")`) as a headless consult in that worktree. When the consult
finishes, relay parses the findings' trailing ```` ```relay ```` block for
`verdict: accepted | rejected` and `reasons: [...]`, records them on the
`KindFindings` entry and on the binding (`LastVerdict`, shown in `status`
as `verdict: rejected` until the next round), and removes the throwaway
worktree. The report is delivered immediately as today; the verdict is its
own payload later. A `rejected` verdict does nothing. Design question 1 is
answered by the headless consult; 2 (second opinion) is not now; 3: the
gate output is passed, labelled. `relay wait --verdict` is a follow-up.

**Tier.** The reviewer runs in a disposable worktree, so it may execute
tests: the consult's tier is `policy.json` `tier.reviewer` when set, else
the candidate's, else `yolo` **for this consult only** (the tree is
throwaway; document it). The role definition still tells it not to edit.

## 5. Pseudocode

Covered by §4. Order at close: gate result -> report queued -> verify
consult started (its `KindAsk` entry logs `verify <id>`) -> delivery. The
consult's own lifecycle is the headless one; only `finishConsult` knows
`verifyRole`.

## 6. Error handling

- No reviewer candidate / all gated / worktree creation failed / runner
  missing: the round closes normally with a `verify skipped: <why>` log
  note; nothing else happens.
- A findings file without the block: `verdict: unstructured`, delivered as
  such -- the planner reads the prose.
- The throwaway worktree is removed on every terminal consult state; a
  leftover under `.worktrees/.verify/` after a crash is the human's to
  `git worktree remove` (README says so).
